iLQ

Radiotherapy dose-equivalence calculator based on the linear-quadratic model.

Contact

Questions, suggestions or a possible error: jebibault@gmail.com

Formulas

Equivalent dose: D₂ = D₁ × (α/β + d₁) / (α/β + d₂)

EQD2: EQD2 = D × (α/β + d) / (α/β + 2)

D: total dose, d: dose per fraction. The model is valid for a dose per fraction between 1 and 6 Gy.

Important

iLQ is intended for research purposes only and must not be used to adapt or modify patient treatment.

Formules

Dose équivalente : D₂ = D₁ × (α/β + d₁) / (α/β + d₂)
